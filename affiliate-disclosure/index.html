Affiliate and sponsorship disclosure

Today, no link on Vlora.live earns a commission and no placement is paid for. Here is how links will be labelled if that changes.

The four labels

  • Editorial — chosen by us, no payment involved.
  • Affiliate link — if you book through the link we may receive a commission. The price you pay is the same.
  • Sponsored — a paid placement, kept separate from editorial choices.
  • Partner — a direct local partner with a signed agreement.
  • External booking site — a plain link to a third-party site with no affiliate relationship.
