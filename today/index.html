            Taverna Te Lilo

            Editorial

            By the port, near Independence Square (Sheshi i Pavarësisë)

            A taverna whose menu is built around traditional Albanian dishes, with seafood, pasta and risotto alongside — and breakfast from the morning.

            Good for
            Traditional Albanian cooking in the centre, a short walk from the port and the north end of the Lungomare.
            Keep in mind
            There is no booking page — just a digital menu and phone numbers — so call ahead for a table in July and August.
            Menu ↗ Directions ↗ Call +355 69 307 2533

            Paradise Beach Restaurant

            Editorial

            Lungomare (Rruga Murat Tërbaçi), on the beach

            A Mediterranean and Italian restaurant on the beach side of the Lungomare; the menu centres on local seafood, with a raw-fish bar and dry-aged steaks.

            Good for
            Tables by the sea on the main promenade — a sunset dinner within walking distance of most central hotels.
            Keep in mind
            It is on the busiest stretch of the promenade, so lively rather than quiet; reserve ahead for a sunset table in high summer.
            Website ↗ Directions ↗ Call +355 69 600 0034 WhatsApp ↗

            Not paid to appear. Details from the restaurant’s website, checked 17 October 2026. Confirm opening times and prices with them.

            Eden by Valza

            Editorial

            Uji i Ftohtë, on the coast road south of the centre

            The rooftop restaurant of the Valza Boutique Hotel at Uji i Ftohtë: modern Albanian and Mediterranean cooking and Albanian wines, looking out over the bay.

            Good for
            A sit-down evening meal with a wide view across the bay towards the sunset, and Albanian wines on the list.
            Keep in mind
            It is an evening restaurant a taxi ride south of the centre, not somewhere to drop into from the promenade; book ahead.
            Website ↗ Directions ↗ Call +355 69 602 2022

            Hotel Vlora International

            Editorial

            Port end of the Lungomare (Sheshi Isa Boletini, Rruga Çamëria)

            A city hotel where the main boulevard meets the Lungomare by the port, with rooms facing the coast or the promenade, an indoor pool and a spa.

            Good for
            A base without a car: the centre, the Lungomare and the ferry port are all within walking distance.
            Keep in mind
            A central hotel on a busy junction: choose it for convenience, not for a quiet beach stay.
            Hotel site · book direct ↗ Directions ↗ Call +355 69 584 3229

            Not paid to appear. Details from the hotel’s website, checked 17 October 2026. Confirm prices and availability with them.

            Sea & Sand Hotel

            Editorial

            Old Beach, north-west of the port (Rruga Sazani)

            A beach hotel a few steps from the Old Beach (Plazhi i Vjetër), with sea-view and family rooms, a restaurant, a garden and free parking.

            Good for
            A quieter base on the Old Beach side of the city, with the sand a few steps away and parking included.
            Keep in mind
            It is 2–3 km from the centre and the Lungomare restaurants — expect a taxi in the evening.
            Hotel site · book direct ↗ Directions ↗ Call +355 69 205 5305

            Not paid to appear. Details from the hotel’s website, checked 17 October 2026. Confirm prices and availability with them.

            Hotel Nimfa

            Editorial

            South coast road, beyond Uji i Ftohtë

            A beachfront hotel on the coast south of Vlora with its own beach, sea-view balconies in most rooms, a restaurant and a beach bar.

            Good for
            A beach stay where a parasol and two deck-chairs on the hotel beach come with the room, and several rooms are sized for families.
            Keep in mind
            You need a car or taxis for the city: the hotel puts it about 6 km, or 15 minutes’ drive, from the centre.

            Hola Taxi

            Editorial

            Vlorë (office on Rruga Sali Vranishti)

            A Vlorë taxi company that takes bookings by WhatsApp or phone, for rides in the city, to beaches and other destinations, and to the airport.

              Flag and Independence Day

              Saturday, 28 November 2026 · Vlorë — in recent years the official flag-raising has taken place at Flag Square (Sheshi i Flamurit)

              The anniversary of Albania’s declaration of independence, made in Vlora on 28 November 1912. A national holiday; in 2026 the day off moves to Monday 30 November.

              Source: Bank of Albania 2026 holiday schedule; National Tourism Agency (1912 declaration in Vlora); Shqiptarja.com report of the 2024 ceremony · checked 17 October 2026

            A typical day in Vlora

            These are photos from past days, not live pictures or today’s light. Press Play above to check current conditions.

            Morning

            Boats leave, the bay is calm

            Boat trips to Sazan and Karaburun depart in the morning when the sea is usually at its flattest. Early walks on the Lungomare are quiet and the light on the mountains is best.

            Midday & afternoon

            Beach hours

            The Lungomare beaches come into their own. Afternoons often bring a sea breeze; the camera shows you the water before you choose a spot.

            Golden hour

            Sunset behind Sazan

            The bay faces the sunset directly. The pier, the promenade and the water all work; the golden-hour window is shown above.

            Evening

            The xhiro and dinner by the water

            The promenade fills after dark. Restaurants, the Ferris wheel and, in summer, open-air music.
