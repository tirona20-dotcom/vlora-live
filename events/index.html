    Flag and Independence Day

    Saturday, 28 November 2026 · Vlorë — in recent years the official flag-raising has taken place at Flag Square (Sheshi i Flamurit)

    The anniversary of Albania’s declaration of independence, made in Vlora on 28 November 1912. A national holiday; in 2026 the day off moves to Monday 30 November.

    • Vlora is where independence was declared, so the city is the traditional centre of the day: in 2024 the President, the Prime Minister and the Speaker attended the official flag-raising in Vlorë.
    • The 2026 programme had not been published when we checked on 17 October 2026. We will add times and places when an official source confirms them.
    • Liberation Day follows on 29 November (day off Tuesday 1 December in 2026); banks and offices close on both.

    Source: Bank of Albania 2026 holiday schedule; National Tourism Agency (1912 declaration in Vlora); Shqiptarja.com report of the 2024 ceremony · checked 17 October 2026

How we list events

  • Source first. Every event links to the organiser or an official source for its date and place.
  • Dated and self-expiring. Each entry has an expiry date; past events disappear on their own, even from pages your browser cached.
  • Honest about gaps. If a date is fixed but the programme is not, we say “Programme not yet published”.
  • No paid ranking. A sponsored event, if we ever carry one, is labelled Sponsored.

Organising something?

Send the name, date, place and a link to the official announcement to vloralivecam@gmail.com. Listing an event with a verifiable source is free.
