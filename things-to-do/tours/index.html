What is on offer

On 17 October 2026 GetYourGuide listed 341 activities for Vlorë and Viator 84. Most fall into a handful of kinds:

  • Boat trips→ Sazan, Karaburun, Haxhi Ali cave, sunset
  • Snorkel & speedboat→ Swim stops along the marine park coast
  • Paragliding→ Tandem flights over the bay or from Llogara
  • Culture day trips→ Apollonia, Berat, Zvërnec, Orikum
  • Food & wine→ Dukat olive groves and farm lunches
  • Off-road→ ATV, quad and buggy tours in the hills

Inland nature trips — canyons, waterfalls and old stone bridges in the valleys east of Vlora — also appear on the booking sites. We have not described them here until we can do so first-hand.

Food & drink

Dukat: olive groves and a country lunch

Below the Llogara road, the Dukat plain is olive-oil country. Agritourism farms serve local dishes, their own oil and wine — a slow lunch on the way to or from the Riviera.

Choosing a tour

  • Hours on the road vs. at the sight. Berat and Apollonia in one day is a long drive; read the itinerary for time on site.
  • Group size and pick-up. Small-group and private options cost more but start from your door.
  • Guide language, entry fees and meals: included or extra?
  • Off-road tours: ask what protective gear and briefing are provided, whether you need a licence to drive, and who is liable for damage. UK advice warns that some rented jet skis and boats lack proper safety equipment; apply the same caution to off-road rentals and choose licensed operators.
  • Cancellation terms, especially for anything weather-dependent.
