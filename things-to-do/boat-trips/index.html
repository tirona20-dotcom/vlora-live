Where the boats go

Three places, one bay

Island

Sazan Island

Albania’s largest island, a former military base at the mouth of the bay and the silhouette on the horizon in almost every camera view.

On camera
Peninsula

Karaburun Peninsula

The wild, roadless mountain arm that forms the western side of Vlora Bay — caves, coves and beaches reached only by boat or on foot.

On camera
Landmark

Haxhi Ali Cave

A high sea cave at the northern tip of Karaburun that boats can enter on calm days; a highlight of most coastal boat trips.

Golden hour

Sunset cruise on Vlora Bay

Leave the waterfront in the last two hours of light and watch the sun drop behind Sazan Island from the water.

Sunset in Vlora happens over the sea, behind Sazan Island, and the bay faces it directly. A short evening cruise — typically ninety minutes to two hours — takes you far enough from the shore to see the whole arc of the city lights come on behind you.

Before you book

How to choose well

  • Shared boat or private? Shared trips are the simplest way to go; a private charter costs more but lets a group choose the stops and the pace — compare per-person costs for your group.
  • Morning is the sea’s calm hour. Most itineraries leave between 09:00 and 11:00. Afternoon breezes are normal in summer.
  • Read what is included. Fuel, skipper, drinks, snorkelling gear and the cave can all be extras. Compare like with like.
  • Cancellations. Weather cancellations are usually refunded or rebooked. Confirm the policy before paying.
  • Where to book. The platforms below list Vlora operators with reviews and free cancellation options; local operators can also be booked directly on the waterfront.

Frequently asked questions

When do boat trips from Vlora run?

Roughly May to October, with daily departures in July and August. Morning departures are the norm because the sea is usually calmest then.

Can I visit Sazan Island on my own?

No public ferry serves Sazan. It is visited on organised boat trips, and landings are controlled. Itineraries can change with the sea state.

Will the boat go inside the Haxhi Ali cave?

Only when the swell allows. Operators decide on the day, so treat the cave as a highlight rather than a guarantee.

How do I know if it is a good day for a boat trip?

Look at the live camera for the real sea state and confirm with the operator, who makes the call. Vlora.live never makes safety assessments.
