Explore Vlora

Places and guides

The island, the peninsula, the mountain pass, the beaches and the table — plus the guides to everything you can book.

  • Beaches→ City sand to wild coves
  • Sazan Island→ Albania’s largest island
  • Karaburun→ Caves, coves, marine park
  • Llogara Pass→ The pass to the Riviera
  • Eat & drink→ Seafood, mussels, local wine
  • Boat trips→ Sazan, Karaburun, the cave
  • Paragliding→ Tandem flights
  • Diving & snorkelling→ Marine park, the Po wreck
  • Tours & day trips→ Berat, Apollonia, Dukat

With children? The Lungomare city beaches have gentle water on calm days, the Lungomare has the Ferris wheel and space to run, the footbridge to Zvërnec’s islet is a small adventure, and a calm-morning boat trip suits most ages.

Boat trips

Sazan Island, the Karaburun coast, the Haxhi Ali cave and sunset cruises — the reason most people come.

Boat trips guide →
Boat trip

Sazan Island & Karaburun boat trip

The classic Vlora day on the water: across the bay to Albania’s largest island, then along the wild Karaburun coast with swim stops.

⏱ Half day or full day

Editorial
Boat trip

Haxhi Ali Cave by boat

A large sea cave at the northern tip of Karaburun, entered by boat on calm days — one of the most photographed stops on the coast.

⏱ Usually part of a 3–6 hour trip

Editorial
Boat trip

Sunset cruise on Vlora Bay

Leave the waterfront in the last two hours of light and watch the sun drop behind Sazan Island from the water.

⏱ 1.5–2.5 hours

Editorial
Boat trip

Private boat charter from Vlora

Your own boat and skipper for a family or group: choose the coves, set the pace, and go as far as Grama Bay on a long day.

⏱ 4 hours to full day

Beach

Lungomare city beaches

The beaches along Vlora’s promenade — walkable from the centre, lined with cafés, and the ones you see on the live camera every day.

Editorial
Beach

Old Beach (Plazhi i Vjetër)

Vlora’s original town beach, on the far side of the port to the north-west — quieter than the Lungomare beaches, with older buildings among newer hotels.

Adventure

The paragliders you see on the camera, flights from Llogara, and snorkelling in the marine park.

Paragliding guide →
Adventure

Paragliding over Vlora Bay

Tandem flights from the hills above the bay, landing on the beach — the paragliders you spot on the live camera on suitable days.

⏱ 2–3 hours

Editorial
Adventure

Tandem paragliding from the Llogara Pass

Take off from the mountains by the Llogara Pass, about a thousand metres up, and glide down towards the Ionian coast — the Riviera alternative to the bay flight.

⏱ Half day

Editorial
Adventure

Snorkelling along the Karaburun coast

Clear water, sea caves and rocky coves inside the Karaburun-Sazan marine park, reached on boat trips that build in swim and snorkel stops.

⏱ Half day or full day

Editorial
History

Culture and viewpoints

Independence, an Ottoman mosque, a hilltop castle, Caesar’s harbour at Orikum and a day out to Berat.

Tours and day trips →
Culture

Old Town, Flag Square and the Muradie Mosque

The historic heart of Vlora: where Albania declared independence in 1912, a 16th-century Ottoman mosque, and the old quarter on the hill.

⏱ 2–4 hours

Editorial
Culture

Kanina Castle and the view over the bay

A hilltop castle above the village of Kanina, south-east of the city: Illyrian origins, walls rebuilt by Romans, Byzantines and Ottomans, and the whole bay at your feet.

⏱ 1–2 hours

Editorial
Culture

Orikum and the ruins of ancient Oricum

At the southern end of the bay: the small town of Orikum, its lagoon and marina, and the remains of Oricum, the harbour Julius Caesar took in 48 BC.

⏱ Half day

Editorial
Culture

Day trip to Apollonia and Berat

North to the ruins of ancient Apollonia, then inland to Berat’s Ottoman old town, a UNESCO World Heritage site — a long, rewarding day away from the coast.

⏱ Full day

Editorial
Beyond the bay

Nature and day trips

Lagoons, a monastery islet and the mountain road to the Riviera.

The Llogara Pass →
Nature

Zvërnec monastery and the Narta Lagoon

A wooden footbridge to a pine-covered islet with a medieval monastery, in a lagoon where flamingos winter — twenty minutes north of the city.

⏱ Half day

Editorial
Nature

Llogara Pass and the Riviera road

The mountain road south of the bay climbs to about 1,000 m through the Llogara pine forest and drops to the Albanian Riviera — a day trip or the start of the next leg.

⏱ Half or full day

Editorial
At the table

Food and drink

Seafood by the water, and olive country at Dukat.

Eat & drink guide →
Food & drink

Seafood lunch on the waterfront

Grilled fish, mussels from the lagoon and a long table by the water: the Vlora lunch that works in any weather.

⏱ 2 hours, ideally more

Editorial
Food & drink

Dukat: olive groves and a country lunch

Below the Llogara road, the Dukat plain is olive-oil country. Agritourism farms serve local dishes, their own oil and wine — a slow lunch on the way to or from the Riviera.

⏱ 2–3 hours
