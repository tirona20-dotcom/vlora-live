Privacy

Short version: nothing tracks you until you say so, and the video player only talks to YouTube when you press play.

Who we are

Vlora.live is operated by Vlora Bay Livecam. Contact: nightowel03@gmail.com.

Analytics (optional)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages and links are useful. Google Consent Mode v2 is configured with all storage denied by default. The analytics script is not loaded at all until you choose “Allow analytics” in the banner. Your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage under vl.consent.v1 and can be changed at any time from the “Analytics cookies” link in the footer. IP anonymisation is enabled.

Live video

The live stream is embedded from youtube-nocookie.com and only loads when you press play. From that moment YouTube’s own privacy policy applies to the player.

Live snapshot and conditions

Camera images and, if a conditions feed is added later, its data file are loaded from our own storage. These requests carry no identifying data beyond what any image request carries (your IP address, which the storage provider processes to deliver the file).

Outbound links

Links to booking sites open third-party websites with their own privacy policies. Where a link is an affiliate link it is labelled as such; see the affiliate disclosure.

Your rights

You can withdraw analytics consent at any time (footer link). To ask about data we may hold — in practice, only email correspondence — write to the address above.
