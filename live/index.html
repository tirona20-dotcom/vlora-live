What you are looking at

Landmarks in the frame

The camera rotates between these views through the day. The full guide explains how to read each one.

Webcam guide →
Island

Sazan Island

Albania’s largest island, a former military base at the mouth of the bay and the silhouette on the horizon in almost every camera view.

Peninsula

Karaburun Peninsula

The wild, roadless mountain arm that forms the western side of Vlora Bay — caves, coves and beaches reached only by boat or on foot.

Waterfront

Lungomare

Vlora’s waterfront promenade and city beaches — cafés, palms, the pier and the Ferris wheel — and the camera’s most frequent subject.

Port

Port of Vlora

The ferry and cargo port at the north end of the Lungomare, west of the centre, where the Brindisi ferries berth and working boats come and go through the day.

Beach

Old Beach (Plazhi i Vjetër)

Vlora’s original town beach, north-west of the port and away from the Lungomare promenade: quieter, with older buildings among newer hotels.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the view keep changing?

One rooftop PTZ camera rotates between selected views so the stream shows more of the bay than a fixed webcam could.

Why is the player not loading automatically?

To keep the page fast and private, the YouTube player loads only when you press play, from youtube-nocookie.com.

Are the pictures on this site live?

No — pictures elsewhere on the site are labelled Bay preview. The live view is the video on this page, streamed from YouTube. To use or embed the camera picture, contact us via the Partners page.
