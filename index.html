About this live webcam in Vlore

Vlora Bay Livecam is a 4K coastal webcam streaming live from Vlore, Albania. The camera captures the curve of Vlora Bay, the Lungomare promenade, changing sea conditions, ferries, port movement, sunsets and clear views toward Sazan Island.

Use this guide to understand the view, then open the full live stream on YouTube for 4K quality, live chat and the latest moments from the bay.

Popular searches: Vlora Bay live cam, Vlore Albania webcam, Vlore live webcam, Lungomare webcam, Adriatic Sea webcam, Sazan Island live view, Karaburun live cam and port webcam Albania.

What you can see

Depending on the preset or angle, the camera can show the bay, open sea, port activity, the Lungomare promenade lined with palm trees and beaches, the sunset line over Sazan Island and the wild Karaburun Peninsula.

Best time to watch

Sunrise, late afternoon and sunset are usually the most rewarding moments. Soft light, Sazan silhouettes, reflections on the water and harbour activity give this webcam its best character.

Paragliders & wide bay

On suitable days, paragliders cross the wide coastal view above the beaches and waterfront.

Port, ferries & boats

Watch ferries, working vessels, yachts and smaller boats moving through the harbour area.

Beaches & coastline

Closer views show the shoreline, swimmers, beach activity and the changing colour of the water.
