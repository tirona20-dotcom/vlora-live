What the camera sees

The live view moves between the Lungomare, the open bay, Sazan, Karaburun and the port. Learn to read it.

Open the webcam guide →
Island

Sazan Island

Albania’s largest island, a former military base at the mouth of the bay and the silhouette on the horizon in almost every camera view.

Peninsula

Karaburun Peninsula

The wild, roadless mountain arm that forms the western side of Vlora Bay — caves, coves and beaches reached only by boat or on foot.

Waterfront

Lungomare

Vlora’s waterfront promenade and city beaches — cafés, palms, the pier and the Ferris wheel — and the camera’s most frequent subject.

Port

Port of Vlora

The ferry and cargo port at the north end of the Lungomare, west of the centre, where the Brindisi ferries berth and working boats come and go through the day.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Vlora.live camera live 24/7?

The camera streams around the clock from Vlora, Albania, on YouTube in up to 4K. Pictures on the website are labelled as previews; the live video is on the Live page and on YouTube.

What can I see on the webcam?

Vlora Bay, the Lungomare, the Adriatic, Sazan Island, the Karaburun Peninsula, the port, ferries, yachts, paragliders and sunsets.

Where is the camera located?

The camera overlooks Vlora Bay near the Lungomare. The precise camera position is not published for privacy.

Is the “Vlora Today” guidance a weather forecast?

No. It follows the time of day, today’s sun times for Vlora and the season to suggest what suits the moment. It is never a marine forecast or a safety assessment — look at the live camera and ask locally before going out on the water.
