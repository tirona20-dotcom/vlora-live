Your trip in four decisions

From the airport to the promenade

  1. 1

    Arrive

    Fly into Tirana — Vlorë’s new airport has no scheduled flights. From Tirana airport it is about 155 km: the airport bus costs 1,200 lek single, a fixed-fare taxi about €105–110. Or sail from Brindisi straight into Vlorë’s port.

    • Getting here →
    • Airport transfers →
    • Ferries →
  2. 2

    Choose your base

    The Lungomare if you want to walk everywhere; the Old Beach, north-west of the port, for a quieter beach base; Uji i Ftohtë, Radhimë and Orikum along the south coast road towards the Riviera; Zvërnec and Narta for quiet and the lagoon.

    • Where to stay →
  3. 3

    Get around

    The waterfront is walkable. Taxis and minibuses cover the coast; a car is worth it for Llogara, the Riviera, Narta and day trips — UK advice calls Albanian roads hazardous, so drive with care.

    • Getting around →
    • Taxis →
    • Car hire →
  4. 4

    Book the few things worth booking

    In July and August, book your room, airport transfer and any boat trip ahead. Everything else — beaches, cafés, the promenade — needs no booking at all.

Planning by season

  • July and August are the busiest months: book accommodation and airport transfers early, expect full beaches and busy evenings on the Lungomare.
  • June and September give you warm sea with more space — the best balance for most visitors.
  • May and October are quieter; boat trips run roughly from May to October, weather permitting, and ferry timetables change with the season.
  • Winter is calm and local. Many beach businesses close; the city, its cafés and the camera’s winter light remain.
