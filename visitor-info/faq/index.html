Arriving

Is there an airport in Vlora?

Not one you can fly into: The new airport near Vlorë has not opened to scheduled flights; the state moved to terminate its concession in August–September 2026. Fly into Tirana. Getting here →

How do I get from Tirana airport to Vlora?

By the Hermes Airport shuttle (1,200 lek single, nine departures a day), by airport taxi (fixed fare about €105–110 per car), by pre-booked transfer, or in a hire car. About 155 km. Airport transfers →

Can I take a ferry from Italy?

Yes — Vlorë has a ferry link with Brindisi. Booking sites listed around four sailings a week for autumn 2026, roughly 6–8 hours; it changes by season. Ferries →

Money and phones

Can I pay with euros?

The currency is the lek. Some visitor businesses accept euros at their own rate; paying in lek is usually cheaper. Cards work in larger places, cash for small ones. Money & ATMs →

Will my phone work without roaming charges?

Not automatically: Albania is outside the EU’s “roam like at home” area. Check your plan, or buy a local SIM (bring your passport) or an eSIM. SIM, eSIM & Wi-Fi →

Should I tip?

Appreciated, not expected: round up in cafés, around ten per cent for good restaurant service. Tipping →

Health and safety

What is the emergency number?

112 for any emergency. Ambulance 127, police 129, fire 128. Emergency & medical help →

Can I drink the tap water?

UK government advice is not to drink tap water in Albania. Buy bottled water. Shopping tips →

Is Vlora safe for visitors?

UK advice says crimes targeting foreigners are rare; take normal precautions against pickpockets in busy places. Road safety is the bigger risk — UK advice describes driving in Albania as hazardous. Safety notes →

Beaches, timing and the camera

Do I have to pay for a sunbed?

Only if you want one. For the 2026 season the municipality capped prices on operator-run beaches at 700 lek and kept around 15,000 m of beach free. Beaches →

When is the best time to visit?

June and September: warm sea, calmer beaches. July and August are busiest; boat trips run roughly May to October. Winter is quiet and many beach businesses close. Things to do →

Is the camera really live?

Yes. One rooftop camera streams Vlora Bay 24/7 in 4K; the site shows a snapshot that refreshes every five minutes, and the full stream plays on demand. Watch live →
