Quick facts

Currency
Albanian lek (ALL) Not the euro. Due for a re-check
Cards and cash
Cards in larger places; cash for small purchases Carry cash outside the city and for small shops, kiosks, buses and beach bars. Due for a re-check
Tap water
Drink bottled water UK travel advice: do not drink the tap water in Albania. Due for a re-check
EU roaming
Not covered Albania is outside the EU’s “roam like at home” area. Check your own operator’s Albania rates before you rely on data. Due for a re-check
Local SIM
Bring your passport Prepaid numbers are registered with a passport or ID card at the shop. Due for a re-check
Vlorë airport
No scheduled flights The new airport near Vlorë has not opened to scheduled flights; the state moved to terminate its concession in August–September 2026. Fly into Tirana. Due for a re-check
