Public holidays (rest of 2026)

Language

The language is Albanian (shqip). In hotels, restaurants and shops that serve visitors you will usually find someone who speaks English, and Italian is widely understood. A few words go a long way:

Përshëndetje

Time, plugs and names

  • Time zone: Central European Time (UTC+1), summer time UTC+2 — the same clock as Italy.
  • Plugs: European two-pin (types C and F), 230 V.
  • Names: Vlorë and Vlora are both correct in Albanian (the second is the definite form); Italians say Valona. You will see all three on signs, tickets and maps.
  • History in one line: Albania’s independence was declared in Vlora on 28 November 1912 — the reason the date is a national holiday.
